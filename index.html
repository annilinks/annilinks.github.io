Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There is no plan to buy, no card and no email needed — only a username and a password.

Who can edit my page?

Only you. Editing needs your password, and your page can only be changed while you are logged in.

What do the stats show?

Page views, visitors by IP address, every link with its taps and copies, where visitors came from, their country, device and browser, and the latest activity. Your own visits are not counted while you are logged in.

What if I forget my password?

When you make your page you get a recovery code — save it. On the login screen, "Forgot your password?" asks for your username and that code, and lets you set a new password. Lost the code too? Write to us from the contact page and we'll help you back in.

What does Pro cost, and can I stop?

Pro is a one-time payment for a month or a year — there is no auto-renewal, so nothing is ever charged without you choosing it again. When Pro runs out your page keeps working on the Free plan.

Can I change my links later?

Any time. Open your page, click Edit, change what you like and save. Visitors see the update within moments.
