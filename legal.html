Terms of use

Linkboard lets you publish a page of links at annilinks.github.io/?u=yourname and see basic statistics about who opens it. By creating an account you agree to what follows.

Your account

  • You choose a username and a password. Keep the password to yourself — anyone who has it can change your page.
  • You are responsible for everything posted on your page.
  • One person may hold more than one account, but accounts may not be traded or sold.

What you may not put on a page

  • Anything illegal in India, or links to it.
  • Malware, phishing, or links that pretend to be a person, brand or payment page they are not.
  • Sexual content involving minors, content that harasses a person, or content that incites violence.
  • Anything that breaks the rules of the sites you link to.

Pages that break these rules can be switched off without notice. If you believe a page was switched off by mistake, write to us and we will look again.

  • Indian law applies, and the courts of India have jurisdiction.

Privacy

We keep as little as the service needs.

If you have an account

  • Username and password. The password is never stored — only a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash of it.
  • Your page. The title, columns and links you save.
  • Login sessions. Only a hash of each session token, with its expiry date.
  • Payments. If you buy Pro, we store the order id, the amount and whether it was paid. Card and UPI details go to Razorpay and never reach us.

When someone opens a page

For every visit we record: the time, which page was opened, the visitor's IP address, the country and city that Cloudflare reports for that address, the device, browser and operating system, where the visit came from, and any link that was tapped or copied. The owner of that page can see this in their stats. Nobody else can.

Who else sees the data

  • Cloudflare runs the servers and database that hold this data.
  • GitHub Pages serves the website files.
  • Razorpay handles payments and holds its own records of them.
  • Google Fonts serves the site's font, and Google's favicon service fetches the small icon next to each link, which means those services see the domains of the links on a page.

We do not sell data, and we do not run ads or advertising trackers.

Your choices

  • You can change or delete the links on your page at any time.
  • Write to us to have your account and its data deleted. We will do it within 30 days.
  • Visit records are kept while the account exists so that stats keep working.

Refunds and cancellation

  • Pro does not renew by itself. You pay once for a month or a year; nothing is charged again unless you buy it again. There is nothing to cancel.
  • Not happy? Write to us within 7 days of paying and we will refund the payment in full, no questions asked. Pro then ends.
  • Refunds go back to the same payment method through Razorpay and usually take 5–7 working days.
  • If a payment is taken but Pro doesn't switch on, write to us — we will either fix it or refund it.
